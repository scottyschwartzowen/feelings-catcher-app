ABOUT THE PROJECT

Welcome to your Feelings Catcher

A feeling is an important signal. It's a flag that says “pay attention to me - not the feeling, you.” You create every feeling in your own body, as a short message to yourself. Usually it's a reminder about something you're doing that you're not fully aware of. Feelings Catcher exists to help you notice that signal the moment it shows up, instead of losing it by the time you finally sit down to reflect.

priority_high
1. Notice It

Stop and notice the feeling. Record it with Feelings Catcher the moment it arises.

sell
2. Define It

Name it in one word - anger, fear, tension, pressure, stress, anxiety.

self_improvement
3. Change It

Notice what you're doing that's generating the signal, and choose to do something different.

You are human, and you're supposed to feel. Feelings only become damaging when we don't use them as intended - as an alert about ourselves. Catching a feeling early, before it turns into loop-thinking or physical tension, is the whole point of this app.
